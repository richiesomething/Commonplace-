Our Story

Hi, I'm Hailey, the owner of Commonplace Stationery. I love pens, journaling, junk journaling, scrapbooking, prayer and scripture journaling, and, of course, stickers.

Somewhere along the way I fell in love with all things stationery, and with commonplace journaling: keeping one notebook for quotes, prayers, favorite books, movies and shows, bucket lists, memories, and all the little bits of life you want to hold on to. That's where the shop gets its name, and this shop means everything to me.

I hope Commonplace Stationery becomes your go-to stationery shop, a place to find your favorite supplies, try something new, and feel at home. Everyone is welcome here.

I give all glory to God for giving me purpose and for helping make this dream a reality. And thank you to my husband, my biggest supporter.

Hailey
