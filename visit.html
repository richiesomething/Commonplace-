Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How long does shipping take?
Orders ship within 1–2 business days from the shop. Standard shipping usually arrives in 3–5 business days. Orders over $40 ship free.
Can I pick up my order in the shop?
Yes! Choose in-store pickup at checkout and we’ll email you when it’s ready, usually within two hours during opening times.
What’s your return policy?
Unused items can be returned within 30 days for a refund or store credit. Opened ink and personalized items can’t be returned, but if something arrives damaged we’ll make it right.
Do you offer gift wrapping?
Always, and always free. Check the gift wrap box in your basket and add a handwritten note; we’ll write it out for you.
Can I try pens before buying?
Please do! Every fountain pen, gel pen and brush pen in the shop has a tester at our pen bar, with scrap paper to doodle on.
Do you make custom stamps or wax seals?
We do. Send us your design (or a sketch of your own pet!) and we’ll have a custom rubber stamp or brass wax seal made in about two weeks.
Do your gift cards expire?
Never. They can be used in the shop or online, and you can check your balance any time by asking us.
Is the shop dog friendly?
Extremely, though usually asleep. The pup is in most afternoons. Please let them nap if they’re curled up, and feel free to say hello if they wander over.
