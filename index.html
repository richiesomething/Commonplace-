Meet the shop dog

Our Head of Naps

Every cozy shop needs a resident napper. Ours is a fluffy, rust-colored pup who greets customers with one sleepy eye, supervises the gift-wrapping table and inspired our logo, our wax seal and our very favorite ink color.
